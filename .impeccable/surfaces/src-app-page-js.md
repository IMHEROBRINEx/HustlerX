# Surface brief: Truss public site and app

Scope: landing (`/`), Why us, About, Sign in (Persuade), and the signed-in app (`/dashboard`: Job Post Search primary, Find People secondary; Operate).

Audience: college students hunting internships and first jobs. Action: sign in and run a first search. Proof: the real mechanism (1st/2nd-degree only, freshness filter, filter counters). No stats, logos, or testimonials exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The campus placement notice board, rebuilt so every lead is a flyer pinned by someone you know. Refuses the gradient-hero-plus-app-screenshot SaaS layout and the dark neon "AI" look.

OWN-WORLD: A committed chart-paper blue pinboard owns the page. Leads are photocopy-paper flyers (white, fluoro yellow, pink, mint) in near-black ink, each held by a coloured push pin and tagged in handwriting with who posted it and their degree. Highlighter yellow marks what Truss understood in a query. Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree UI, Kalam handwriting for pin tags only. Depth comes only from paper lifting off the board.

STORY: A student sees that Truss finds internship posts from people in their own network, believes the leads are fresh and reachable, and tears off a tab to start searching.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue board. Left: the headline flyer, "Internships with someone to ask," with tear-off tabs along its bottom edge as the primary action ("Start searching"). Right: a pinned cluster of three labelled example flyers, each with a degree tag and posted-time stamp, plus a sticky note explaining the 1st/2nd-degree rule. Tape-strip nav across the top.

FORM: Placement Board, candidate 1 of 7 (Impeccable's pick), seed key 54f46ba5. Signature interaction: flyers sit slightly crooked and straighten as they lift on hover or focus; a tear-off tab pulls down when pressed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
